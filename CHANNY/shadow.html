    Solo Leveling: 10 Sung Jin-Woo's Shadows, Ranked According to Strength







    Tank

    Tank was introduced to Solo Leveling readers when Sung Jin-Woo encountered a Red Gate. Initially, the Guild exploring the Dungeon was under the assumption that they entered a C-Ranked one, which was originally true until their location changed to a far more dangerous one after entry. To their surprise, they discovered that the Red Gate was home to powerful Ice Bears and Ice Elves.

    Later on, Kim-Chul, the A-Rank hunter leading the party, quickly excluded party members lower than C-Rank from following them. However, Jin-Woo could not ignore this golden chance to level up. He ventured into the Ice Bear territory and killed all of them, including their Boss Leader. After killing the Ice Bears, Jin-Woo added the Boss Leader to his Shadow Army, naming him "Tank" due to his raw attack power.







    Kaisel

    Kaisel is a Wyvern Sung Jin-Woo encountered while leveling up in the Demon territory. In the Demon Territory, Jin-Woo leveled up, but for some reason, he was unable to use his Shadow Extraction skill on Demons. However, this changed when he met Kaisellin, Baran’s flight mount.

    After a sensational battle, Jin-Woo defeated Demon King Baran, while Iron, his subordinate, eliminated Kaisellin. However, the protagonist used his Shadow Extraction ability to revive Kaisellin as a shadow before naming him "Kaisel." Although Kaisel’s Strength wasn't highlighted in the Webtoon, his flight ability and enhanced senses place him at the top of the Jin-Woo army's ranking.







    Jima

    Jima isn't shown much in Solo Leveling, but its strength is unmistakable. Sung Jin-Woo encountered the magical creature in a raid Ahjin Guild participated in. Initially, the Hunters couldn't defeat this beast due to its strength and magical prowess. Wielding a trident, Jima resembles a sea serpent due to its large size.







    Iron

    In the Ice Bear Saga, Tank wasn't the only trophy Sung Jin-Woo gained; Iron was on the list. After the hero's party ventured into the Red Gate and were attacked by Ice Elves, they split up. Their A-Rank leader disallowed any member that was lower than C-Rank from following them in clearing the Dungeon so they don't become dead weight.

    After an unsuccessful raid, Kim Chul, an A-Rank White Guild member, discovered that the neglected party members were unharmed and even enjoyed their stay at the Dungeon. Enraged and jealous of this fact, he tried to attack Jin-Woo but was killed by Igris. The manhwa's main character then resurrected the Hunter as "Iron."







    Greed

    Greed was the Shadow of Hwang Dong-Soo, a powerful S-Rank Hunter from Scavenger Guild. Hwang Dong-Soo bred an immense hatred for Sung Jin-Woo based on the suspicion that he killed his brother. However, after discovering that Jin-Woo was far stronger than him, Dong-Soo tried getting rid of him using a roundabout method: Thomas Andre.







    Tusk

    Initially, Tusk was the Boss leader of the High Orcs at the Hunter Guild Dungeon. During the Dungeon's raid led by an A-Rank Hunter, the situation went south after the High Orcs revealed themselves. After an intense battle, the hunters discovered that they could not fight anymore, and they decided to surrender.

    After surrendering, they met the High Orcs Shaman leader, Kargalgan, who revealed his intention to kill the Hunters to entertain himself. However, Sung Jin-Woo intervened, fighting against Kargalgan and killing him; he then resurrected Kargalgan as Tusk. Tusk is an immensely powerful Shadow with magical abilities, ranging from Gravity magic to Telepathy.







    Igris

    Igris was a red knightly creature that Sung Jin-Woo fought after a hard-pressed battle in a dungeon. In this Dungeon, Jin-Woo encountered many difficult obstacles, such as Necromancers that kept on summoning soldiers to attack him. However, after discovering the Necromancers' ploy, Jin-Woo killed all of them and earned the skill of Shadow Extraction.

    He uses this skill to revive the red knight he fought at the Dungeon's entrance, naming him "Igris." So, Igris was one of Jin-Woo's first Shadows. Like other powerful members of the protagonist's army, Igris continued growing in strength till the end of Solo Leveling and gained the ability to speak upon reaching Marshal Rank.







    Beru

    Beru was a powerful minion Jin-Woo obtained during the "Jeju Island Arc". Beru’s strength while being an Ant King is on par or even greater than S-Rank Hunters. He single-handedly defeated Goto Ryuji, a man revered as Japan's strongest Hunter, and struck fear into the heart of an S-Rank Hunter, who declared Beru as being impossible to defeat.

    However, Beru's free reign ends when he meets Sung Jin-Woo, who surpasses him in strength and speed. After Beru becomes Jin-Woo's shadow, his healing abilities are revealed in the Webtoon.







    Kamish

    The United States tried to convince Sung Jin-Woo to join their ranks as a Hunter. However, before Jin-Woo agreed, he made a request to meet Kamish, a Dragon that even Thomas Andre couldn’t defeat easily. Once this came to pass, Jin-Woo used Shadow Extraction on Kamish, reviving the powerful monster.

    Despite being under Jin-Woo’s dominion, the Dragon still managed to act independently, almost killing the humans surrounding his master. Sadly, Kamish couldn't last long as Jin-Woo's shadow since he had been dead for a long period. However, from the shadow's brief show of power, he is easily Marshal Rank.







    Bellion

    Bellion is the only shadow that Jin-Woo didn’t face since he was among Ashborn’s final gifts to the protagonist for the Monarch War. Previously, Bellion served Ashborn, the Shadow Monarch, as a leading figure in his army. However, when Ashborn officially appointed Sung Jin-Woo as his successor, Bellion and the rest of Shadow Monarch's army surrendered to their new master.

    Bellion's strength was also proved in his short bout with Beru. Bellion easily subdued the Marshal-ranked shadow using his extendable sword. Still, Beru tried to attack Bellion under constraint, but his tenacious attacks did not faze the latter, who appeared unscathed from the attack.
